# Words (from styleguide: usage dictionary)

## Differentiate
- Access levels, roles and permissions: each user has an access level and a role; each role includes a set of permissions.
- *Create* (new; opposite of delete) vs *add* (existing; opposite of remove). *Download* vs *export*. *Open* vs *expand*.
- *Must* (required), *should* or *is recommended* (recommended), *can* (optional). Never "need to".

## Use
- *can*, not "be able to", "allows you to" or "enables you to".
- *dialog*, not dialog box, modal, modal dialog, modal window, pop-up, pop-up window or window.
- **Select**, not press, click, touch or tap. *select* and *clear* for checkboxes. *enter*, not type.
- *earlier* and *later* for versions, not lower, older, higher or above.
- *go to*, not navigate to. *use*, not utilize. *turn on*, not enable. Instead of *disable*: turn off, hide, make unavailable or inactive, remove. Instead of *downgrade*: change to earlier or lower.
- Instead of *respectively*: be precise. Drop *easily* and *simply*.
- *following* (not below) and *previous* (not above) for images and examples, or a direct link.
- Projects are *private* (not personal). Keep "Personal" only where it is the exact UI label.

## Phrases
- "Complete the fields" for filling in a form: no explanation for self-explanatory fields; explain a single field in the step itself; explain many fields in a bulleted list.
- For FAQs and troubleshooting, use: "You can find answers to common questions or solutions to known issues in the broader ELITEA documentation."
- Never: "This page is about…", "This guide explains…", "please note", "at this time".

## Time-anchored words
- Avoid: as of this writing, currently, does not yet, eventually, existing, future, in the future, latest, new, newer, now, old, older, presently, at present, soon.
- Exceptions (user decisions): *new* meaning "newly created" ("a new chat is private"); *latest* meaning position ("the latest message"). All time words are allowed in release notes and press releases.
