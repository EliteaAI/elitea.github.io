# Language (from styleguide: language — general, voice, grammar, punctuation, capitalization)

Apply to new and edited text. In untouched text, report only (category "style").

## General & voice
- Be clear, focused, precise, direct and approachable; friendly, respectful and natural.
- Write only facts: objective, verified and verifiable. Write from the customer's side: what they can do and gain.
- Be specific: facts and achievable goals. Simple words, short sentences, paragraphs of five sentences or fewer.
- US English. Consistent terms, sentence structures and formatting. No ambiguous words.
- No plagiarism: link to the source or rephrase with a reference.

## Grammar
- Active voice; passive only to focus on who receives the action. Present tense; future only for future actions.
- Prefer positive constructions. No double negatives, no exceptions to exceptions.
- Pronouns: *you*/*your* for the reader; *they* for a generic person; *it*/*its* for an organization, company or single client; *I* only in FAQs. Check that each pronoun points to the right noun.
- No contractions. No possessive ('s) on proper nouns or product names (ELITEA's).
- Plurals: regular forms; APIs, not API's; no plural on a unit after a number (64 GB); no "(s)": choose one form or write "one or more".
- Abbreviations: spell out once, at first use on the page: Model Context Protocol (MCP). Countries known by their acronym (USA) need no spelling out. Never make an abbreviation possessive.
- Plural of acronyms: the styleguide conflicts (7.2 "APIs" vs 9.3 "do not make plural"). Until the user decides, keep what the page has and report it.
- Avoid phrasal verbs. Parallel structure: the same structure and tense for similar items, such as list items.
- Order in a sentence: location, reason, condition, then action.

## Punctuation
- No Oxford comma: "agents, pipelines and skills".
- Lists: the same punctuation for all items; a period only for full sentences; items with several sentences punctuate each one.
- Double quotes for quoted text, punctuation inside them; single quotes for a quote within a quote.
- Country acronyms: two letters with periods (U.S., U.K.); three or more letters without (USA, UAE).
- No en dashes (–): hyphen (-) in words, em dash (—) between words, without spaces. No ellipses.
- Parentheses sparingly and short; turn long ones into a second sentence.
- Avoid question marks and semicolons; no exclamation marks. Slashes only in URLs and file paths.

## Capitalization
- Products: the producer's official capitalization. UI text: exactly as the UI shows it.
- Glossary and index terms in lowercase unless proper nouns. Tables: sentence case for contents, headings, labels and captions.
